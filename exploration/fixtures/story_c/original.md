Red Mesa, Arizona — March 4, 2026 — Lumenarc Dynamics today announced the Helios Array, a planned network of compute campuses, and closed a $400 million Series D financing led by Northwind Capital.

The company said the Helios Array is planned to reach 1.8 GW of compute capacity by 2029.

The Helios Array's first campus is in Red Mesa, Arizona.

Chief Executive Officer Mara Ellison said the financing funds construction of that first campus.
